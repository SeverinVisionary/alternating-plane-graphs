# Prior art, checked 2026-09-06

Does anyone else already have these results? The question matters because the
whole point of this deposit is to settle open problems from
[Althöfer, Haugland, Scherer, Schneider & Van Cleemput, *Alternating plane
graphs*, *Ars Math. Contemp.* **8** (2015) 337-363][src].

**Answer: no published or publicly deposited settlement of Conjecture 10.1,
10.2 or 10.3 was found.** Method and evidence below, so the check can be
repeated rather than believed.

  the full PDF, not the abstract.

## Two things that looked like leads and were not

* A web search returned **arXiv 2409.19398** as a 2024 revision of the source
  paper by the same five authors. **It is not.** That identifier is an
  information-theory paper on arbitrarily varying channels. Checked by fetching
  it.
* The authors' page says a *"revised version ... exists and is under revision"*.
  The page's own last update is **1 December 2014**, so that sentence describes
  the pre-publication state of the 2015 paper itself, not a later development.

## Standing caveat

Citation indexes lag and do not cover every venue; a settlement could exist in a
thesis, a seminar, or a journal none of the three indexes reach. What is claimed
here is that **three independent indexes, the arXiv, and the authors' own page
show nothing**, as of 2026-09-06. The source paper has been cited twice in
eleven years, so the field around it is very small -- which cuts both ways: a
result is unlikely to have been quietly published, and equally unlikely to be
noticed if it were.

The original authors have **not been contacted**. They are the one source that
would settle this definitively.
