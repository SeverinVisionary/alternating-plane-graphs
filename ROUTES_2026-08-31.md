# Route brainstorm: every way to close Conjecture 10.2, priced

Dated 2026-08-31. Breadth pass over full-problem strategies, written after the
solver-core diagnosis and the first CNF witness (order 17, 4.2 s). Nothing
here is a result. Where a route depends on a bet, the bet is stated, with a
calibration gate that prices it cheaply before real compute.

## The shape of the problem, in one paragraph

The 26 targets are exactly the composition gaps of the published block
arithmetic. Composing `k` blocks of orders 21-24 (increments 18-21) yields
orders `3 + sum` in `[3+18k, 3+21k]`: `k=2` gives 39-45, `k=3` gives 57-66,
`k=4` gives 75-87, `k=5` gives 93-108, and from `k=6` (orders >= 111) the
ranges overlap forever. The gaps between those bands are `46-56`, `67-74`,
`88-92`, `109-110` -- precisely the target set. So the problem is not "search
26 large objects"; it is "widen the increment set". One new increment-22
block (order 25) alone closes **11 of 26** targets (46, 47, 67-69, 88-91,
109, 110, within the `t <= 4` budget); the frozen triples in
`block_arithmetic.py` close all 26.

## Measured constraints any route must respect

- Direct CDCL search certifies order 17 in 4.2 s and stalls at order 20
  (240 s, satisfiable profiles). The wall is the `n3! n4! n5!` relabelling
  orbit, not formula size (order 46 builds in 6.3 s).
- Exhaustive plantri-style generation is structurally closed: `~(256/27)^n`
  below the pruning. Priced at `10^32` x order-13 cost for order 46.
- Infeasible profiles refute fast: `(20,10)`, `(20,7)`, `(22,8)` are
  `ENCODING_UNSAT` in 4-9 s. Refutation scales differently from search.
- The block profiles `(25,11)`, `(28,12)`, `(29,12)`, `(31,12)` are now
  CNF-encodable, with all four published blocks passing as models.

## Routes, ranked

### R1. Block completion search in CNF -- the priority lane

Search the small object, compose the big ones. `(25,11)` first: order 25 is
88 darts (the smallest open block profile), the Section-8 port theorem forces
`t = 1` there (finite-use, budget still allows up to four uses), and one hit
closes 11/26. Then the portable `t=0` triple `(28,12)/(29,12)/(31,12)`.

What the CNF block lane still needs before its negative results mean
anything: the socket structural constraints (whites on the two hexagons, the
socket cycle alternation), the `t` gate, and the socket canonical normal form
ported from `BOOLEAN_SOCKET_CANONICALIZATION.md`. Candidates travel only
through `blocks.validate_block`, the nine closures, and both verifiers.

Bet: a block exists at one of these profiles. Not guaranteed -- the published
19-embedding opening scan found none, but that scanned only public embeddings.
Kill criterion: structure-complete `ENCODING_UNSAT` at every admissible small
profile; that would be a checkable theorem about those profiles (see R6) and
would redirect to R2/R5.

### R2. Local surgery: grow a known witness by +1/+2 vertices

The targets sit directly above known orders (45 known, 46 wanted; 66 known,
67 wanted; ...). A move that inserts one or two vertices into an existing APG
while repairing alternation locally turns any known witness into a ladder.
The repository already has the move machinery (three/four/five-edge
rematchings, near-opening scans, `frontier_anneal`); what is missing is
vertex-count-changing moves (edge subdivision + rewire, vertex split, face
split) and an anneal over them with the defect function already in use.
Cheap to build on what exists; heuristic, so time-boxed rather than killed.
The order-44 House-of-Graphs witnesses and every composed order 39-45 map are
seeds.

### R3. Symmetric quotient search -- **KILLED 2026-09-01**

Impose a `C2` (then `C3`) automorphism and search the quotient: an order-46
map with an involution is ~23 vertices of data, which is inside the measured
frontier. Calibration gate before any encoding work: compute automorphism
groups of the 88 public APGs (orders 17-44). If a solid fraction are
symmetric, the bet that symmetric witnesses exist at 46+ is reasonable; if
nearly all are asymmetric, deprioritise. Half a day to calibrate.

**Verdict: killed.** The calibration ran (`automorphism_census.py`): 21 of the
23 published APGs held here are rigid, the only two with a nontrivial
automorphism are the order-17 maps, and every map at orders 26-42 is
asymmetric. See
[`results/logs/route_calibration_20260901.md`](results/logs/route_calibration_20260901.md).

### R4. Canonical-construction search -- **narrowed to SMS 2026-09-01**

The principled fix to the symmetry wall: SAT-modulo-symmetries (minimality as
a propagator) or a native canonical-augmentation grower that builds the map
face-by-face with alternation enforced during growth -- the thing plantri
cannot do because it fixes triangulations first. This is the largest
engineering item (order weeks, not days) and the one with the best direct
shot at order 46-56 if R1 stalls. **Narrowed.** The nearest existing tool, Brinkmann's homomorphism-principle
map generator (arXiv:2408.16512), generates rotation systems with a prescribed
degree sequence at millions per second and needs no isomorphism rejection on
rigid graphs -- but its author states it is not useful for genus 0, and its own
embedding count `(1/2) prod (deg(i)-1)!` is astronomical at the order-46
profile. So this route is SAT-modulo-symmetries (Kirchweger-Szeider), not
homomorphism-principle generation, and SMS should be prototyped against the
measured order-17/order-20 pair before it is built out.

### R5. A repeatable strip -- **THE STRIP EXISTS, verified 2026-09-01**

A cylindrical strip of circumference/increment `c` that composes with itself
(alternation-compatible on both boundaries) plus cap pairs covers arithmetic
progressions; with caps at `c` consecutive sizes it covers whole bands. The
46-56 gap is 11 consecutive orders -- one strip with `c <= 11` and a run of
caps would close the biggest gap at a stroke. This is mathematics rather than
compute: the independent review is the right first mover on whether such strips
can exist (the published blocks are already two-port; the question is
self-composability and small `c`).

### R6. The refutation side, taken seriously

`ENCODING_UNSAT` at every feasible `r` of one target order would refute the
conjecture -- **if** the encoding is faithful to Definition 2.1 rather than to
the repository certificate contract. Today it is not known to be: at minimum
the no-repeated-vertex-on-a-face clause and implicit connectivity assumptions
must be re-audited against the paper before any UNSAT is promoted beyond
"this encoding, this profile". If that audit closes, the route is: per-`r`
DRAT proofs, verified checker, and the lex-leader break finally earns its
keep (symmetry breaking pays on the UNSAT side -- measured: it costs 24x on
the SAT side). Fast small-order refutations (`(22,8)` in 9 s) suggest this
scales differently from witness search. This route is also the honest hedge:
the program should not assume the conjecture is true.

### R7. Orthogonal multipliers (apply to R1/R3/R6)

- Portfolio: feasible `r` x solver seeds x cores. Witness search on
  symmetric satisfiable instances is high-variance; parallelism attacks
  variance directly. Cheapest first move on any cloud worker.
- Cube-and-conquer: split on the triangle-label or vertex-0 neighbourhood
  variables; standard for exactly this instance class.
- CP-SAT/ILP pricing probe: one day to test whether circuit-style global
  constraints beat clause-level propagation at order 20; adopt only on a
  >5x win.

### R8. The zero-compute route

Author and House-of-Graphs existence-vector poll. Required by PRIOR_ART.md
before any public claim anyway; doing it early also prevents spending a month
on an order a private construction already closes. One email, no compute.

## Recommended allocation

1. R1 until structure-complete, with R7 multipliers -- it is the program's own
   lane, the smallest search, and 11/26 hangs on one object.
2. R3 calibration gate and R8 immediately (both ~free).
3. R5 question to the independent review now (staged brief committed alongside).
4. R4 begins when R1 either stalls structure-complete or eats its profiles;
   R6's faithfulness audit proceeds in the background since R1's UNSATs
   inherit it.
